# 无字原版立绘提示词

用途：生成不含题字、不含印章的角色原版立绘。默认按第一版生成方向使用：重视水墨氛围、晕染质感和设定页观感，不额外加入清晰版强化线稿提示。

## 通用风格关键词

```text
vertical full-body xianxia character portrait, Chinese ink-wash concept art,
expressive dry-brush black linework, hand-drawn sketch, semi-transparent watercolor wash,
low-saturation palette, pale matte paper background, faint oversized ghost sketch behind character,
sweeping ink brush arcs, flat minimal background, flowing Hanfu / Daoist robes,
wide sleeves, subtle ornamental accessories, elegant rough line economy,
character design sheet, full-body standing pose, generous empty space,
muted cyan blue gray ochre ink palette, soft paper grain, watercolor blooms
```

## 通用约束

```text
No readable text, no Chinese calligraphy, no phone UI, no watermark, no logo,
no photorealism, no 3D render, no modern elements, no neon glow,
no overly detailed background, no crowded composition, no copied reference character.
```
